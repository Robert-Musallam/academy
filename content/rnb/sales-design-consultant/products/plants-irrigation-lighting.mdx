Plants: curb appeal, property value, climate-appropriate (desert), low-water/low-maintenance options, privacy and shade, expert design and placement. Irrigation: water efficiency, healthy plants, custom zoned systems (trees, shrubs, turf, planters), WiFi smart controllers, drip irrigation, high-quality parts. Lighting: extends living space, safety/security, highlights landscape/architecture/trees/water, low-voltage LED, custom design, clean concealed wiring.
