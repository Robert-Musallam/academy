Standard fencing: ¼" or ½" vertical bars, wide spacing, see-through, minimal privacy, standard latch. Modern fencing: 2"×1" steel panels, ~1" gap, maximum privacy/security, clean high-end look, stronger, increases value. Standard gate: bars, see-through, no key lock, basic. Modern gate: solid steel panel, minimal gap, key lock, custom look. Why modern: privacy, security, curb appeal, built to last, adds value. 3-year warranty on materials and labor.

**Pricing:** fencing by LF, gates by unit, includes professional installation.
