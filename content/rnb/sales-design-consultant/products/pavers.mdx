45mm for patios, walkways, pool decks, side yards (foot traffic); 60mm for driveways and heavy vehicle traffic. Why pavers: 25-year warranty; more affordable than concrete for a high-end look; stained/damaged — replace that paver; flex with temperature, won't crack like concrete; endless styles/colors; increases property value.

**Pricing:** sold by square footage; Sailor-style edger included in the sqft at no extra charge. Pro tip: pavers outperform concrete in beauty, durability, value.
