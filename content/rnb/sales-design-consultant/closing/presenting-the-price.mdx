## Earn the price conversation

First-call close is the goal. Before presenting price, confirm the customer's vision and explain the proposed products, installation process, warranty, design benefits, and why RNB is different. Check that the scope matches what the customer wants. Do not skip education because a customer asks for a quick number.

Review the full proposal and reinforce the value before revealing the investment. Point to the features that address needs the customer actually named. Speak clearly, give the customer room to respond, and listen for the concern behind a question.

Present financing alongside every proposal using current company-approved information. Do not invent financing terms or promise approval. Explain the available information accurately and help the customer identify what they want to explore.

State the 25-year product warranty for turf, pavers, and fencing; 3-year labor warranty; and Bull lifetime warranty on kitchen equipment. RNB has 15+ years in the industry and is licensed and insured.

## Ask for the business

After the full proposal, value explanation, price, and financing presentation, ask the customer whether they are ready to move forward. Acknowledge any concern and use the five-step objection pattern. A same-day drop is at most 10%, and may be offered only after the full proposal has been presented and value reinforced. Do not lead with a discount.

<PhrasesToUse>

"We've reviewed how this design supports the way you want to use the space, how we'll install it, and the warranty. Let's review the proposal and the financing options together."

"Are you ready to move forward with this design?"

</PhrasesToUse>

<WhyItMatters>

The customer can evaluate the investment when they understand the scope, installation quality, and result they are buying.

</WhyItMatters>
