Pattern: Acknowledge → Understand → Educate → Reframe → Close. "An objection is not a rejection."

Acknowledge the concern without arguing. Understand what is holding the customer back. Educate with relevant product and installation facts. Reframe around the customer's goals and long-term value. Close by asking for a decision or an agreed next step. Do not skip discovery or lead with a discount.

<WhyItMatters>

The pattern helps you respond to the real concern while respecting the customer.

</WhyItMatters>

<PhrasesToUse>

"An objection is not a rejection."

</PhrasesToUse>
