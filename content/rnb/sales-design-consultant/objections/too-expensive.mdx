"It's too expensive." Means: don't see enough value / comparing prices. Response: understand; "If price wasn't the concern, would this be the solution you'd want for your home?" Quality materials, proper installation, results that last save money over time.

Use the full pattern: Acknowledge → Understand → Educate → Reframe → Close. First-call close is the goal. A same-day drop is at most 10% and comes only after the full proposal and reinforced value; financing accompanies every proposal.

<WhyItMatters>

Find the underlying concern before choosing the response. Do not turn every objection into a discount.

</WhyItMatters>
