"We need to get other estimates." Means: want to compare. Response: compare more than price — materials, installation process, warranty, reputation; "we have been in the industry for over 15 years."

Use the full pattern: Acknowledge → Understand → Educate → Reframe → Close. First-call close is the goal. A same-day drop is within the territory-approved cap (current default 10%) and comes only after the full proposal and reinforced value; financing accompanies every proposal.

<WhyItMatters>

Find the underlying concern before choosing the response. Do not turn every objection into a discount.

</WhyItMatters>
