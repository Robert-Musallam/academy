The manual requires you to get square footage from the sample diagram and build the estimate in HCP. Complete this exercise with your manager in the company's normal training process.

1. Identify the customer vision and project scope.
2. Record readable dimensions and determine the specific selling unit for each item.
3. Have the manager review the diagram and quantity takeoff, including waste, edger, delivery, and required length additions.
4. Build the proposal in HCP with the right materials and methods, multiple options, and the value of each.
5. Review installation, warranty, design benefits, long-term value, and company differences before presenting price.
6. Present financing alongside the proposal.
7. Ask the manager to review the completed estimate and record the HCP exercise sign-off in Academy.

Academy does not connect to HCP. Its manager sign-off records completion of this external supervised exercise.

<WhyItMatters>

A correct takeoff becomes useful when the estimate includes the right materials, methods, and quantities.

</WhyItMatters>
