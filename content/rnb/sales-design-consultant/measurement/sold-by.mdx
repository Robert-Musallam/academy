LINEAR FEET: 6" CMU walls; paver edger; poly tubing; electrical wire; fencing; Romex wire (add 14' if tapping from panel); AWG wire for spas (add 14'); edging; gas lines (add 8' to any linear footage needed). Remember: measure twice, sell with confidence.
