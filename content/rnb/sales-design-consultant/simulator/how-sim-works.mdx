The Simulator unlocks only after M5 passes. Choose a homeowner persona in the available tier and conduct a text appointment. There are 6 core personas: 2 warm, 2 standard, 2 hard. Each has multiple scenario variations, starting with 3 per persona (18 appointments). Project scope, customer priorities, and constraints vary while each persona retains its tier and core behavior. Hidden information is revealed only when earned through the conversation.

## The 100-point rubric

- Introduction & rapport — 10
- Discovery before products (vision, use, pain points) — 15
- Measurement/pricing accuracy (correct unit, edger, waste, tons) — 10
- Education before price — 20
- Objection handling (5-step pattern) — 15
- Close attempt (asked for business, drop + financing used correctly, never dropped early) — 20
- Next step secured (agreed follow-up, personal number) — 10

Pass = total ≥ 75 AND every dimension ≥ 50% of its weight. The grader returns a score and reason for each dimension, three quoted trainee lines to fix, one thing done well, and a pass boolean.

First-call close is the goal. Educate before price. Reinforce value after the full proposal before offering a same-day discount of within the territory-approved cap (current default 10%). Present financing with every proposal. Secure the next contact and give your personal number.

Each run allows a maximum of 40 turns. Each trainee may start at most 10 runs per calendar day. Every run logs prompt/completion tokens and estimated cost.
