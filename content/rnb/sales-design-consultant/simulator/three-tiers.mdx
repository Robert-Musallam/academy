Complete one passing run at each tier: warm, standard, hard. Warm scenarios are a turf backyard with dogs and spouse present, or a referral paver patio with fire pit. Standard scenarios are a patio cover with an absent spouse, or an outdoor kitchen customer who wants financing. Hard scenarios include competitor turf and a fencing/gate price shopper.

A run passes at total ≥ 75 with every dimension ≥ 50% of its weight. Review the three quoted lines to fix and the strength noted in your grade. Practice the missing skill before another run. M7 unlocks only when all three tiers are passed. Limits: 40 turns per run and 10 runs per trainee per calendar day.

<WhyItMatters>

Practice needs to transfer across different customer concerns; a single warm pass does not complete M6.

</WhyItMatters>
