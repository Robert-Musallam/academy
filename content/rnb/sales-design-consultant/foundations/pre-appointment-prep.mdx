## Before you leave

Review the appointment details and the customer's stated project. Know where you are going and allow time to arrive prepared. Review what is known, then list questions you still need to ask. Do not substitute assumptions about the customer for discovery.

Bring measuring tools, a way to draw and take notes, project photos, material samples, and a competitor turf sample. Be ready to explain the relevant product, installation process, warranty, and why RNB does things differently. Review the specific selling units, minimums, delivery rules, and utility-length additions for the expected scope.

Rehearse the opening, then plan to listen. The 7-step consultation begins with introduction and discovery; it does not begin with a price. Measure accurately while building rapport. Prepare options that connect to how the customer wants to use the space.

Know the company facts: 15+ years in the industry, licensed and insured. State the lifetime turf warranty; a 3-year warranty for pavers and other materials; 3-year labor warranty; and Bull lifetime warranty on kitchen equipment. Bring the current company-approved financing information. Do not invent a rate, payment, eligibility decision, or financing promise.

## Ready-to-go check

- Appointment details reviewed and discovery questions ready.
- Measuring and drawing tools, photos, and relevant samples packed.
- Installation, selling units, and warranty language reviewed.
- Financing information available for the proposal.
- Personal phone number ready to share; a way to record an agreed next contact.

<PhrasesToUse>

"Tell me about the project you're envisioning." Start with their goals before describing your solution.

</PhrasesToUse>

<WhyItMatters>

Preparation lets you give the customer your attention, measure carefully, and explain value without improvising facts.

</WhyItMatters>
