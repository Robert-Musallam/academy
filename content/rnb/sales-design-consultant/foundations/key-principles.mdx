Key principles: listen more than you speak; ask the right questions; offer ideas, don't be an order taker; build rapport and trust; educate before discussing price; respect the customer's time; treat every home like your own; be confident, professional, genuine. "People don't buy the cheapest price… they buy the best value from the people they trust."

<WhyItMatters>

Listening and thoughtful questions reveal what a useful solution needs to accomplish.

</WhyItMatters>

<PhrasesToUse>

"Tell me about the project you're envisioning."

</PhrasesToUse>
