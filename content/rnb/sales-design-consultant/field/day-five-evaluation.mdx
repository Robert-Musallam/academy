Day 5 — final evaluation: run real appointments with supervision, handle questions, build design and proposal, present price confidently, do your drops, performance review, strengths & weaknesses, refine pitch.

The manager evaluates these 8 items, records strengths and weaknesses, and records a pass decision. A completed form is not a trainee self-assessment. Field completion also requires the ride-along checklist and HCP exercise sign-off after M6 passes.

<WhyItMatters>

The final evaluation establishes whether the trainee can apply the process under supervision.
