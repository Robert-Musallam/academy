The manager reviews the completed sample-diagram estimate in HCP and marks the HCP estimate exercise complete in Academy. The exercise uses the right materials and methods, correct selling units, and manager-reviewed quantities.

M7 is complete only after M6 passes and the manager completes the ride-along checklist, a passing Day 5 evaluation, and the HCP exercise sign-off. Academy records the sign-off and does not access HCP.

<WhyItMatters>

The final sign-off closes the loop between measurement, proposal preparation, and manager review.
